# Pelo Finance — Terms of Use (draft)

**Version:** 2026-10-26-v1  
**Status:** Prototype draft; legal review required before production.

Pelo Finance is a hackathon-stage cash-flow planning prototype. It helps users organise information they choose to enter and explore estimates. It is not a bank, payment service, accounting guarantee, credit bureau, lender, or professional financial, tax or legal adviser.

Users must provide information they are authorised to use, keep account credentials confidential, and not enter bank passwords, OTPs, payment credentials or other secrets. Do not use the prototype for consequential lending, insurance, employment, pricing or eligibility decisions.

Forecasts are estimates based on available records and may be inaccurate. PeloScore is illustrative and is not a validated credit score or creditworthiness decision. Bank/wallet connections and events shown as simulations are not real provider integrations.
