# Pelo Finance — Privacy Notice (draft)

**Version:** 2026-10-26-v1  
**Status:** Prototype draft; verify actual provider settings and obtain legal review before production.

## Information stored

The service may store name, email, password hash, business profile, opening balance, manually entered financial transactions, account settings, simulated connection records, notifications, Peloyame usage counts and policy-choice records. Synthetic public demo profiles are separate from private account records.

## Purposes

Data is used to authenticate users, display account-specific summaries, calculate estimates, operate features, prevent abuse, record policy choices, and support account export or deletion.

## Service providers and transfers

The current architecture uses Vercel for the web frontend, Render for the API, Supabase/PostgreSQL for deployed persistence and Groq for Peloyame responses when the user opts in. Providers may process data outside Botswana; verify the actual regions, contracts, and applicable cross-border transfer safeguards before production.

## Choices and requests

Peloyame AI processing is optional and can be disabled in account settings. Users may export account data or request account deletion through available account controls, subject to applicable legal retention and the separate retention of backups/provider logs.

This notice is not a legal compliance certification. Confirm the deployed data map, retention periods, lawful bases, user rights process and cross-border transfers with qualified counsel before production.
